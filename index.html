The Event

We are pleased to announce that #a11yTO Conf is returning to the Barbara Frum Atrium at CBC on October 22 & 23, 2026.

Mark those calendars, save the date, and get ready for two amazing days of learning and networking with fellow attendees from around the world.

This year, we are excited to take Conf back to what started it - a focus on technical talks with clear, actionable takeaways. No matter what your role is, we will have a talk for you.

Tickets

We have extended our Early Bird ticket sales by one week to give you a little extra time to take advantage of the $100 savings. Make sure to grab your ticket by end of day August 7, 2026.

We know travel isn't always possible, so we're excited to once again offer a virtual conference experience — thanks to our Diamond Sponsor, CBC.

Buy your tickets.

Already have an in-person ticket? Email info@a11yto.com and we'll help you switch to virtual. Not sure you can attend in-person or virtually? You can always switch from virtual to in-person, or vice versa (space permitting), up until two weeks before the event.
