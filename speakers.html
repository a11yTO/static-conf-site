We are super excited about the this years lineup of speakers.

Jason Thompson

Jason is a Lead Digital Accessibility Specialist focused on strategy, helping organizations move beyond compliance to build sustainable, scalable accessibility practices. With a background in development and hands-on testing, he bridges the gap between technical execution and organizational change–turning accessibility from a checkbox into a capability.

Fabian Herrera

Fabian Herrera is a Senior Software Engineer on the Core Front-end Platform team at Top Hat and a certified professional in web accessibility (CPWA). He is deeply passionate about digital accessibility, believing we should create seamless, delightful experiences for all end-users–not just products they merely tolerate. Beyond engineering, Fabian advocates for embedding inclusive design principles into our everyday internal communications and team culture.

Jeremy Rivera

Jeremy Rivera is a Full-stack Software Engineer and Developer Advocate with extensive experience in writing and public speaking.  He is an active learner with an aptitude towards Cloud technologies such as AWS, Kubernetes and Salesforce.

Jeremy holds a Certified Professional in Web Accessibility (CPWA) certification from the IAAP and has presented several talks on digital accessibility at conferences like A11yNYC, A11y San Diego, Tampa Devs, Axe-Con, DroidCon, and Accessibility Camp Bay Area.

Claudio Luís Vera

Claudio Luís Vera is a digital accessibility consultant, speaker, and writer who explores how design and technology either enable or disable people. His work spans inclusive design, legal risk, assistive technology, and the ethics of AI. He has written for UX Collective and Smashing Magazine, spoken at CSUN, M-Enabling, and the ICT Symposium, and co-hosts Slop Happens, a podcast on the unintended consequences of technology.

Dusty Levenberg

Dusty Levenberg is an Accessibility Product Manager at Riot Games, specializing in embedding inclusive design across complex digital ecosystems. With a background in compliance spanning startups, enterprise organizations, and government, Dusty brings a systems-level perspective to accessibility that bridges regulatory requirements with player experience.

Ian Lloyd

Ian has been banging on about accessibility things since 2000 as a developer, in one form or another. He has spoken at CSUN, Pixel Pioneers (UK), and State of the Browser (UK). Ian creates tools and bookmarklets to help accessibility professionals, and other tools for developers in general. Ian also runs a11y-tools.com.

Justin Stockton

Justin brings a unique combination of technical depth, accessibility expertise, and product management experience to his work. Having walked the path from developer to accessibility engineer to product manager, he understands the challenges organizations face at every level. His approach to accessibility is pragmatic and results-oriented: he believes that sustainable accessibility programs require not just technical compliance, but cultural transformation, embedding accessibility into every phase of the development lifecycle, from initial design concepts through deployment and maintenance.

James Steinbach

James Steinbach is a front-end engineer with more than 15 years of experience building websites and applications for organizations ranging from agencies to developer-platform companies. His work focuses on modern CSS, design systems, accessibility, web performance, and the ever-evolving capabilities of the browser platform. Throughout his career, James has been an advocate for writing front-end code that is both user-friendly and developer-friendly: fast, accessible, maintainable, and enjoyable to work with.

Patrick Fox

With nearly 30 years of web development experience and 20 years in the marketing agency world, Patrick has partnered with dozens of Fortune 500 clients to craft digital experiences that are as accessible as they are elegant. A passionate advocate for accessibility-first thinking, Patrick brings a combination of technical depth and real-world scale to the challenges of modern UI development - always in pursuit of solutions that are simpler, more robust, and built to last.

Jessica Roache

Jessica Roache is Senior Manager, Global Corporate Communications at Ubisoft, where she spearheads Ubisoft's global accessibility communications program. Driving this 360-degree initiative led to her recognition as winner of the 2023 GAConf "MVP Award for Unsung Hero," an award recognizing behind-the-scenes efforts with big impact. Working cross-functionally to embed accessibility in Ubisoft's communications, marketing and events across its games and services, Jessica's passion is to empower everyone to drive accessibility improvements in their own sphere of influence.

Tait Hoyem

Tait Hoyem is a Computer Science graduate looking to make accessibility better at its technical core: building AT protocols, efficient screen readers, and firmware-level TTS capabilities. Tait’s goal is to get technology out of your way so that you can build what you want.

Roy Samuelson

Roy Samuelson ensures blind people enjoy watching videos. He holds a patent portfolio in Audio Description, authored A Voice Actor's Guide to Audio Description Performance, and hosts The ADNA Presents podcast. Through Parity Endeavors, he consults with media and technology companies on human vs. AI voice decisions and how to build accessibility into production from the start. He treats accessibility as storycraft, not compliance.
